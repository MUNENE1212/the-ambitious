## Addendum — two remaining gaps now closed

**1. The `uncommenced` tab is now fully enumerated.** It contains exactly **one page** (page 2 returns HTTP 404) with **6 instruments**: the Constitution, Prevention of Fraud (Investlements) Act (1977/1), the Medical Practitioners and Dentists (CPD) Regulations, the Pharmacy and Poisons Rules (LN 186/1957), and the Use of Poisonous Substances Act (1957/23). **No Co-operative instrument.** Since an assented-but-uncommenced Act would appear precisely here, this closes the decisive gap for Q1.

**2. The two subsidiary legislation instruments under Cap. 490 are now identified** — and they are *ethics* instruments, not rules or bye-laws:

- **Legal Notice 172 of 2003** — *The Co-operative Societies Code of Conduct and Ethics*, published Kenya Gazette Vol. CV–No. 106 on 31 October 2003, **commenced 31 October 2003**, revised by the 24th Annual Supplement (LN 221 of 2023) on 31 December 2022. **Made under the Public Officer Ethics Act**, not under Cap. 490's rule-making power in s.91.
- **Legal Notice 173 of 2003** — *The Ethics Commission for Co-operative Societies: Procedures for the Administration of Part IV of the Act*, same gazette and commencement dates, same2023 revision.

Both are **in force**, despite being listed under the site's "repealed" tab (that listing reflects supersession of the original 2003 versions by the 2023 revision).

### The consequence for Q5 is significant

I searched the full text of LN 172: it contains **zero occurrences of "bye-law" or "by-law"**, and **zero** of "dividend", "bonus", "contribution" and "reserve". Its one "model" hit is about officers being role models. It is purely an officer-conduct code.

So: **Kenya Law publishes no Co-operative Societies Rules and no model bye-laws whatsoever.** The only co-operative subsidiary legislation on the national legal database is the ethics pair. The **Co-operative Societies Rules** — which under s.91 must prescribe registration forms (s.6), fees (s.91(2)(r)), annual returns (s.91(2)(q)) and the **reserve-fund percentage (s.47(2))** — are therefore **not accessible from any source I can reach**. That last point is the operationally important one: the reserve percentage your by-laws must specify cannot be verified from Kenya Law.

### Incidental confirmation for Q2

LN 173's definitions section designates the **"Senior Deputy Commissioner for Co-operative Development"** as the "administration officer" and secretary to the Ethics Commission — confirming that senior Co-operative administrative offices sit under the Commissioner (s.3), not under a separate "Registrar".
